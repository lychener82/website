Los Simpson: What Is, What Was And What Could Be

17.07.2026, 6 pm

Super Mini Athens

In June 2024, I was invited to exhibit work in a group exhibition curated by Erlend Hammer at Haugar Kunstmuseum, titled Arbeidstittel: The Workers. The invitation coincided with a notice of termination for Borgenheim Rosenhoff's former exhibition space in Rosenhoff, Oslo. It was proposed that Haugar Kunstmuseum's collection acquire a franchise license from Borgenheim Rosenhoff, and that the gallery, both physically and administratively, be relocated to the premises of Haugar Kunstmuseum for the duration of said museum exhibition. Super Mini Athens was commissioned to continue programming Borgenheim Rosenhoff exhibitions and events as part of the exhibition. The interior of the gallery was dismantled, reinstalled and opened in the forecourt of the museum as Borgenheim Rosenhoff Pavilion by Super Mini Athens née Mojito.
The original presentation of the group exhibition Los Simpson (2022) was one of these program entries. It had been presented as a single poster: a promotional image for a fictional feature film, an adaptation of The Simpsons, in which each participating artist was cast in the role of one member of the Simpson nuclear family. The opening took place on June 20, 2024. Zero people attended, and nine Instagram users watched an announced livestream.
In this presentation, the 2024 group exhibition Los Simpson is shown alongside archival material from the realization of the original presentation, together with a bar installation featuring a Swiss artist cast as Markus Torsvik, bar manager of the Borgenheim Rosenhoff Pavilion by Super Mini Athens née Mojito in Haugar Kunstmuseum.
Super Mini Athens (formerly Mojito) is an artist duo and traveling exhibition project, founded in Berlin in 2023.
